# Fractional replay — tsmom

Not investment advice. Generated by `python -m price_forecast.replay.portfolio`.

TSMOM average. Rule: mean 1[ret>0] over 2/4/8/13/26/52 wk  
Sizing rule: `SizingRule(kind='tsmom', target_vol=0.4, vol_com_days=60, lookbacks=(2, 4, 8, 13, 26, 52))` (frozen in `price_forecast/replay/sizing.py`)  
Data: `btc-usd-daily.csv` 2018-01-01 → 2026-09-27. Replay starts 2018-04-29 (first day every candidate in the comparison can trade, so all share one HODL).  
Cost 0.15% of traded notional per fill. Default fill: **next_day, 10 bps slippage**. Equity marked daily; Sharpe from Sunday-to-Sunday returns.
