## 5. Selection bias

Ledger `ledger.jsonl`: 20,461 rows, 17,394 scored, 424 unique specs. Cross-spec Sharpe SD 0.34 (annualised).

Deflated Sharpe: the probability the true Sharpe beats the best Sharpe expected from N skill-less trials. Above 0.95 is the usual bar. The ledger counts only the CrashGate breed; the earlier SMA search and bakeoff are not in it, so the real N is higher.

| 10 | 0.53 | 1.18 | 0.981 | 1.15 | 0.976 |

| 1,000 | 1.10 | 1.18 | 0.601 | 1.15 | 0.566 |

CSCV probability of backtest overfitting over 175 distinct ledger return streams, 432 weeks in 16 blocks (12,870 splits), next-week fills as searched:

| PBO | Median logit | Picked strategy loses OOS | Median IS Sharpe of pick | Median OOS Sharpe of pick |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.42 | 0.21 | 3.1% | 1.40 | 0.81 |

PBO is the share of splits where the in-sample winner ranks at or below the median out of sample. Near 0 means the search's ranking carries over; 0.5 means picking the winner is a coin flip.
