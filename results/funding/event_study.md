# Funding-rate event study

Not investment advice. Measurement only: no strategy, no backtest.

- Generated by `python -m price_forecast.funding` (design: [preregistration.md](preregistration.md)). Sections marked "hand-written" were added after the run.
- BTC prices: Coinbase daily closes 2018-01-01 → 2026-09-27 (`data/btc-usd-daily.csv`).
- Funding: `bybit_btcusd` from 2018-11-15, `binance_btcusdt` from 2019-09-10. 8h prints, daily rate = 3 × mean of the UTC day's prints; feature = trailing 7-day mean.
- Horizons 7d, 14d, 21d, 28d: close(D+h)/close(D) − 1, entered at the signal day's close.

## Verdict

- **FAIL**. Primary family: 12 cells (3 features × 4 horizons), top-minus-bottom decile spread, Holm-adjusted at α = 0.05.
- No cell survives Holm. Smallest raw p: `bybit_btcusd_f7` 14d, spread -5.16%, t = -1.45, raw p = 0.146, Holm p = 1.000.
- Spread cells with raw p < 0.05: 0 of 12 (≈0.6 expected by chance).
- Secondary family (z-score slope): 0 of 12 cells Holm p < 0.05.
- Adds information beyond trailing 28d return (needed for a strategy step): **no**.

## Reading (hand-written after the results; not pre-registered)

- **Under the pre-registered bar, funding fails as a timing source.** All 12 spreads are negative,
  which matches the contrarian prior, but none has |t| above 1.45. The 12 cells use the same days at
  overlapping horizons, so 12 negative signs are far from 12 independent confirmations.
- **The spread comes from the bottom decile, not the top.** Negative-funding weeks (decile 1) were
  followed by strong returns, for example `bybit_btcusd_f7` +13.1% over 28 days against +4.7%
  unconditional. The top decile is close to unconditional. The pattern is not monotone: deciles 6–8
  are strong and deciles 9–10 are weak, and |ρ| ≤ 0.32 everywhere. For a long-only book this means
  high funding has not reliably flagged bad periods to sit out.
- **The effect is old.** Before 2021 the within-period spreads have t from −1.7 to −2.9. That sample
  is 473–772 days and includes the March 2020 crash, when Bybit's 7-day funding fell to about −148%/yr just before the rebound.
  From 2021 onward every |t| < 0.8. These subperiod t-stats are uncorrected.
- **The full-sample deciles carry hindsight.** With deciles ranked on past values only, the Binance
  and venue-average spreads flip to positive, so part of the full-sample result depends on knowing
  the eventual range of funding levels.
- **A lead to test separately, not a finding.** Controlling for the trailing 28-day return makes the
  funding slope more negative: 7 of 12 cells have raw |t| ≥ 1.96, the strongest being
  `bybit_btcusd_f7` 14d at t = −2.66. Funding correlates 0.44–0.50 with the trailing 28-day return, so momentum may be masking
  a contrarian funding component. After Holm across those 12 tests, the best adjusted p is 0.094
  (computed after the results, not part of the generator), so it is not significant. If pursued, it
  needs its own pre-registration, ideally on data this study has not used.
